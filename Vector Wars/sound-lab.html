Sound lab

Every sound in the game, played by the game's own synth. Slow motion does two things at once: it plays sounds lower and longer. Here you can take just the lower part (detune, in semitones, at normal speed) or just the longer part (stretch, at normal pitch). Set it for all sounds in the bar below, then fine-tune single sounds on their cards. When something sounds right, send Claude the settings at the bottom.
