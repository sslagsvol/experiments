Vector Wars style guide

Every color, type style, game element, formation and animation, side by side and drawn the way it appears in the game. Play the game · Plan · Edits

Keep this page in sync. Any change to a color, font, size, HUD style, glow, formation or animation must update this page in the same commit.
  1. live sections read their values straight from src/config.js, src/formations.js and styles.css. After a change, reload and check they still look right. New values need a label or card added here.
  2. manual sections are hand-drawn approximations of shaders and effect code. Edit them by hand when the look or behavior changes.
  3. If you can't update it right away, add an item under Style guide debt in EDITS.md so it isn't forgotten.

Enemy formations live · enemyFormations.js

Generated by the real src/enemyFormations.js at 140 units, top-down with the front row (nearest the player) at the bottom. spread is how well a squad keeps its shape while charging; every enemy turns inward once it's level with the army.

Enemy types live · ENEMY_TYPES shapes manual

Stats from ENEMY_TYPES in src/enemyFormations.js. Shapes are drawn to match the enemy shader in src/enemies.js. Squads are sized by expected damage (est), so a squad with brutes or bombers has fewer units. Grunts are 80–90% of every squad (squadMix()).

Sound effects live · src/audio.js · SFX

Synthesized with WebAudio, so there are no audio files. Tap to play each sound. Volumes and rate limits come from SFX in src/config.js; rate limits keep big battles from turning into a wall of noise. In slow motion every sound plays lower and longer. The mute choice is saved per device.

Glow and post-processing live · BLOOM

Bloom only picks up pixels brighter than the threshold, which is why HDR colors above 1.0 glow and gate text stays readable. Tone mapping is off; OutputPass converts to sRGB.
