Vector Wars: art directions

The four directions considered early on. B (neon swarm) was chosen. D's voxel shattering survives as the destruction style for large objects.

A. Tabletop

Wooden meeples on a board-game map. Gates are playing cards.

B. Neon swarm

Units are points of light. Scales to 10k+ units.

Chosen

C. Risograph

Two-ink print on paper, flat top-down. Looks like an indie game.

D. Voxel

Chunky cube soldiers that shatter into blocks when they die.
