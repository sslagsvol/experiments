Gate sounds

Candidates for the good gate sound, the one that plays when your army passes a + or × gate. They use the same synth as the rest of the game (sine sweeps, filtered noise, echo) instead of the chiptune arpeggio. In context plays a few bullet hits and an enemy pop first, the way you'd hear it mid-run. Tell Claude the letters you like.

Volume

Slow motion plays everything lower and longer, as the game does when you're close to losing. Back-to-back plays the sound twice 0.4s apart, like passing two gates in quick succession.
